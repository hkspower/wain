# Catalogue import — OpenCart export

`90` rows in the export became **46 products**. The rows are size and
colour variants; the site sizes garments by category, so variants collapse into one
product each. Generated by `sporta-web/scripts/import-opencart.py`.

**Nothing was read from the Orders or Order Items sheets except unit counts per
product id.** Those sheets hold 119 real customers' names, emails and phone numbers,
which do not belong in a public catalogue.

### Stock is not enforced

37 of 46 products have 2 or fewer units left, and
**the site has no stock control** — it will keep selling them after the last one goes.
Either keep the shelf topped up, hide the thin ones, or ask for stock enforcement to
be added to `create_order`.
