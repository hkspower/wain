Colour variants

Four, in order of preference. Reach for the first that the background allows — never reach past one that would have worked.

Primary · on lightThe default. Anything from white to sand-100.
Primary · on darkUnchanged. The white keyline is doing the work here, which is why it exists.
One colour · sea-700Where a process limits you to one ink. Keyline knocks out to the paper.
Knockout · on brandSolid white on a brand field: embroidery, signage, a coloured band.

On photographs

Primary or knockout, never one-colour. The keyline is what keeps the mark legible over an uncontrolled background — a flat one-colour mark disappears into a busy image.

Greyscale

Check it. Sea-600 and coral-700 land close together in luminance, so a naive desaturation flattens the pin into the country. Use the one-colour variant instead of converting the primary.
