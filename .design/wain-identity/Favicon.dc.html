What to ship

One SVG favicon carrying the pin-with-؟, plus a 16px PNG of the solid pin for browsers that rasterise badly. The site already serves a scalable icon.svg — this replaces its artwork, not its plumbing.

The rule

Below 32px the mark loses the country. Below 24px it loses the ؟ too. Simplifying is not a different logo — it is the same pin, drawn for the distance it is read at.
