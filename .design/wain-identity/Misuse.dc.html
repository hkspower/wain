Misuse

Every one of these is something that will otherwise happen. The mark's geometry, its colours and its keyline are fixed — the only thing anyone should be choosing is which variant and how big.

Do not stretch or squash. The country is a real shape.
Do not rotate. The pin points down because gravity is the metaphor.
Do not recolour. Sea and coral are the mark; the palette's other ramps are not.
Do not add effects — shadows, bevels, glows, outlines.

Do not drop the pin. Without it the mark is a map, not a brand.
