App icon

The installed icon is the mark on a flat ground — never the gradient country on a gradient field. Android crops the maskable variant to whatever shape the launcher wants, so the safe area is not optional.

any · 192 / 512 White ground, mark at 65% of the canvas.
maskable · 512 Mark at 52%. Everything must sit inside the dashed circle — the 80% safe area.

Why two files

A launcher that crops an any icon to a circle cuts the pin's point off. A maskable icon padded for that crop looks small and lost when shown uncropped. They are different drawings of the same mark, and both ship.

Ground

Flat white, matching the manifest's background_color and theme_color. Not the sea gradient: a gradient behind a gradient mark kills the white keyline that separates them.
