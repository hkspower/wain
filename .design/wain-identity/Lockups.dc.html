Lockups

Three, and only three. The mark sits to the right of the wordmark in the horizontal lockup, because Arabic reads right-to-left and the mark is what the eye should meet first.

Proportion

In the horizontal lockup the mark's height equals the wordmark's cap height plus its descender — roughly 1.25× the type size. In the stacked lockup the mark is about 2.2× the type size. Set these once and never re-eyeball them per placement.
