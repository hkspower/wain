وين؟ · Brand identity

The mark

Kuwait, drawn literally, with the pin planted on Kuwait City and the Arabic question mark inside it. The country says where you are; the pin and the ؟ say where next — which is the whole product in one shape.

Primary · full colour

Construction

Drawn on a 84×92 viewBox. Every edge — country, pin and glyph — carries the same 3.5-unit white keyline, which is what lets the mark sit on a photograph or a coloured band without a container. The ؟ is stroked at 3, not set as type, so it never depends on a font being present.

The two gradients

sea-400 → sea-600
#57b0e3 → #2277b4
coral-500 → coral-700
#ef4d43 → #b9241b

Both are vertical and both run light-to-dark, so the pin reads as lit from the same direction as the country beneath it.
