# SEO keys, deep search (2026-11-19)

Owner request: "make deep search seo keys". This extends `SEO-KEYWORDS-KUWAIT-2026-11-18.md` (100 phrases) with phrases that Kuwaiti sportswear retailers use on their own pages, and says what the search could not establish.

## What the search could and could not show

- **Not found:** search volumes for Kuwait. Google Keyword Planner gives only rounded ranges to accounts without spend, and no source returned Kuwait figures for these terms. A volume check needs a Google Ads account with Kuwait set as the location, or an Arabic keyword tool with Kuwait selected. Until then, every phrase below is a candidate, not a ranked one.
- **Not found:** Google Trends for Kuwait, and any study of Kuwaiti Arabic search terms for sportswear.
- **Found:** how the retailers name their categories. Their own pages are the best evidence of what shoppers type, because the shops are paid to match search.
- **Found:** the general guidance that Kuwaiti shoppers search in Gulf-dialect Arabic, that many expatriates search in English, and that mixed Arabic-English and transliterated phrases occur. Use the phrasings below, not literal translations of the English.

## Notes for using these

1. Do not stuff them. Each category page should have one primary phrase in its title and first heading, and the others only where they read naturally.
2. The Arabic is written in Gulf-leaning, shop-facing wording as the competitors use it. Check with a native Kuwaiti reader before the copy goes live.
3. The existing 100-phrase list stays the guide-page plan (cafes, restaurants, gyms). These new phrases are for the shop's own category pages and product copy.
4. Before spending effort on any of them, get the volumes: Google Ads with Kuwait as the location, or an Arabic keyword tool with Kuwait selected. Drop phrases with no volume and promote ones with volume.
