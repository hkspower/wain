# Full scan, 2026-11-19 (read-only)

Owner: "full scan / full improve cataloge full dynamic / CI/CD". Scope: the live site `www.sporta.com.kw` and the repo at snapshot `6471d47d`, in nine lanes. **All nine lanes returned a result; none came back empty.** Nothing was changed on the live site, no order, form, sign-in or POST was made, no secret value or customer data is printed here, and every scratch project on the VPS was deleted (the two catalogue-baseline projects `-b2` and `-c` had delete requests queued and were still listed at the lane's last check: look again). The repo HEAD moved to `40314444` during the scan and the working tree held other agents' edits, so repo findings are against the snapshot.

Verification: the findings rated high or new-medium were each checked by two skeptics. No finding was disputed (see "Disputed findings").

## Verdict

**The shop is healthy and serving exactly what was deployed**: 27 of 27 add-on and static files equal commit `88bccf54` byte for byte, 44 page loads show no JS error, failed request, sideways scroll or mixed content, 84 of 84 sitemap URLs answer 200, `SCAN_DONE bad=0 warn=0`, the repo has no secret and `npm audit` reports 0 vulnerabilities. Nothing exploitable was found.

Ranked items: **4 high, 22 medium, 50 low**, plus info items (listed compactly). The five that matter most:

1. **`/shop` and its category pages shift 0.44-0.55 (CLS) on first load** (F-01, new). SCAN-1110 had it near 0.
2. **The Arabic phone home page is slow: LCP 6.2 s, TBT 0.99 s** (F-02, new), against 3.4 s on 2026-11-11; the hero downloads three images.
3. **Card payments are still impossible** (F-04): KNET credentials are the shipped placeholders, T-Pay has test credentials only, and no order has been placed since 2026-09-30 (M-10). Owner steps, not a deploy.
4. **14 active products have no photo and are hidden** (F-03), and 14 products carry placeholder stock (M-08).
5. **The catalogue is not yet "fully dynamic"** (M-14 to M-22): no product list for a crawler without JavaScript, category order and all copy hard-coded, no sort or filters, a PDF with no options and no text layer, and a live refresh that misses renames and category changes.

CI/CD: the repo has no workflow and none is to be added (CLAUDE.md item 2). The scan found nothing that changes that; release gating is the existing `vps-deploy.mjs` plan/apply path.
