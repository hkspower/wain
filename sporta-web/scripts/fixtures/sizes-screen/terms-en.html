1. Who we are

Sporta (سبورتا) is a sportswear retailer in Kuwait, selling online at www.sporta.com.kw. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +965 2209 1914 or by email at cs@sporta.com.kw.

2. Orders and prices

All prices are in Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD) and include any applicable taxes. Checkout shows the products, any discount, the delivery fee and the total before you confirm your order. If a page is out of date, the current total is shown at checkout.

Online-payment orders are confirmed after successful payment. Cash-on-delivery orders are placed through checkout and paid to the driver on delivery. If an item turns out to be unavailable after you order, we will contact you and refund any amount already paid for that item.

3. Payment

The payment step shows the methods currently available for your order. Cash on delivery is available within Kuwait. When online KNET or card payment is enabled, it is processed on the bank’s payment page; Sporta does not see, handle or store your card or KNET details.

Cash on delivery is for orders inside Kuwait. Please have the amount ready, as drivers may not carry change. If an online payment is taken but you cannot find your order, contact us with your order or payment reference so we can investigate.

4. Delivery

Orders inside Kuwait are delivered within 24 hours of confirmation, for a delivery fee of 1 KWD to all areas of Kuwait, with updates sent on WhatsApp. Delivery depends on the address details you give us — governorate, area, block, street and building — so please check them before paying.

Online checkout covers Kuwait. For GCC and Middle East orders, message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange delivery and confirm the cost with you first.

5. Returns and exchange

Submit a return or exchange request on our Returns page within 14 days of delivery using your order number and checkout phone. You can return to that page to check the saved request status; WhatsApp is available for help. We arrange free collection for eligible requests after review. Please keep the item unworn, unwashed, in its original condition and with its tags attached. Some hygiene-sensitive items may have special conditions; we review each request under the applicable consumer rules and do not limit your legal rights.

Approved online-payment refunds go back to the card or KNET account used to pay. For cash-on-delivery orders, our support team confirms the refund arrangement with you after reviewing the request. Contact us about hygiene-sensitive items so we can confirm the applicable conditions.

6. Using this site

Product photographs, text, the Sporta name and the Sporta logo belong to us or to the brands we stock, and may not be copied for commercial use. Brand names are the property of their respective owners.

Please do not attempt to disrupt the site, place fraudulent orders, or access accounts and data that are not yours.

7. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows when the wording last changed. The terms in force are the ones published when you place your order.

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Kuwait, and the courts of Kuwait have jurisdiction over any dispute.
