**webcode-2 - `/pay/pay.php` cannot auto-submit under the production CSP (lane: medium confidence; latent today)**
- Files: `sporta-web/dropin/php-cbk/pay.php:152,156`, `sporta-web/public/.htaccess:394`, `sporta-web/dropin/php-cbk/.htaccess`, `sporta-web/public/sporta-admin-brands.js:963`.

- Basis: header text and CSP semantics, not a browser run.
