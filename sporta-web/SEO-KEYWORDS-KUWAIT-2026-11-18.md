# SEO keys for Kuwait: 100 keyphrases, 10 groups of 10 (2026-11-18)

Owner request: "make seo keys for kuwait 100 keys - top 10 cafe, 10 restaurant, 10 top gyms, 10 sportswear, top diet restaurant 10, top ...". The message ended after the fifth group, so groups 6-10 (supplements, running, ladies fitness, CrossFit / boxing / martial arts, padel / football / team sports) are my choice of what fits Sporta's shoppers: change them freely.

**What this is.** Search phrases people in Kuwait type, in English and in Arabic (Arabic first for this shop), written the way they are searched (Gulf spelling where it differs). No search volume, difficulty or ranking was measured: nothing here is from a keyword tool. Check each phrase in Google Search Console (Performance, once the shop has data) or Google Keyword Planner before building a page around it, and drop the ones with no searches.

**How to use it.** `Use` says what kind of page serves the phrase: *shop pages* (sportswear and its kinds) are where Sporta sells; *guide / blog* pages (cafes, restaurants, gyms, diet food, ...) catch people who are not looking for clothes yet and lead them to the shop. A guide that ranks must be a real, useful guide: a "best cafes in Kuwait" page needs your own honest picks (the lists here name no venue on purpose; "top 10" is a page you write, not a keyword you can paste). Put the Arabic phrase in the Arabic page's title, first heading and first paragraph, and the English one on the `?lang=en` page. The SEO screen in /backends (SEO Editor) is where a page's title and description are set.
