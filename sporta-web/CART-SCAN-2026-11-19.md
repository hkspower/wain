# Cart scan, 2026-11-19 (bag page and bag drawer)

Owner: "scan cart". Read-only scan of `/cart` and the bag drawer on the captured live storefront (the real compiled client, capture of
2026-11-03) under this repo's add-on files, Arabic and English, 320 / 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 px. 52 page loads: an empty bag, the three-line
bag of the cart suites, and a mixed bag of seven lines (a long English and Arabic name, a product on sale at 8.000 from 10.000, two sizes of one
product, a line at its stock maximum, a line whose product the shop no longer lists). Script: the session scratchpad `cartx/scan.mjs` (not
shipped; it uses `scripts/lib/cart-ux-live.mjs`). The fixes below are in the repo, built and tested, **not deployed**.

## Result

**What holds (nothing to change):** no sideways scroll in 52 loads; every button and link in a line is 44 x 44 px or more; no clipped text; no text
under 12 px; page and drawer agree line for line (name, size, unit price, quantity, line total, "Maximum available" note); the app re-prices a
listed product from the shop's list (a bag line stored at 4.000 for a product that now costs 5.000 shows 5.000); Arabic amounts carry their
bidi marks and the sale line reads "كان 10.000 د.ك. (−20٪)"; the empty bag says so and offers "Back to shop" in both places; one `h1`
("Your bag" / "حقيبتك"); a sticky Subtotal + Checkout bar on the page; the drawer shows Subtotal, the payment line, Checkout and Back to shop.

**Two defects, both mine (PRICES-1118, live since `deploy-20261118T164133Z-3aa05cc`), found and fixed:**

| # | Severity | What | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| CS-01 | High | **"Original total" and "You save" on the bag page were doubled** whenever a line was on sale. The compiled app keeps the closed drawer's copy of every line in the DOM while the page is open, and the script counted both sets: one line of 2 pieces at 8.000 (was 10.000) showed *Original total 40.000, You save 8.000* (right: 20.000 and 4.000); the mixed bag showed 158.000 / 8.000 for a bag whose lines add up to 75.000 and whose saving is 4.000. The app's own Subtotal was always right. | **Fixed** in `sporta-features.js` (`LINES[].totals`: only the page's lines feed the page summary). Proof: `npm run test:prices-1118` (71) has a real-compiled-page check with the closed drawer in the DOM, mutation-checked |
| CS-02 | Medium | **A second copy of the line total.** A line of two or more pieces showed `KWD 20.000  KWD 20.000` in its quantity row: the app already draws the line total beside the stepper, and the script added its own "Line total" tag, which also pushed the remove button onto a row of its own. | **Fixed**: the tag is added only where the build draws no total of its own (it never is, on the compiled page). `test:prices-1118`: the page as the app draws it, and a mutant |

**Left as they are (low or informational):**

- **CS-03 (low, accessibility).** Every line's buttons are named alike: "Decrease quantity", "Increase quantity", "Remove" (Arabic the same), with no product name,
  so a screen-reader user in a seven-line bag cannot tell which line a button belongs to. Not changed: add-on CSS and tests select those buttons by their
  exact label (`button[aria-label="Increase quantity"]`), so a name change has to be made in both at once. Suggested: append the product name to the
  label in add-on JavaScript and move the selectors to a `data-` mark.
- **CS-04 (info).** A bag that holds one of the 14 products hidden from the shop since 2026-11-19 (no photo file) keeps that line with its stored
  price; the app cannot know the shop stopped listing it, and its product link now answers "not found". The order path still accepts it by design (a
  missing photo or SKU hides the card only; a missing name or a price of 0 would be refused as `unavailable_<slug>`). If the owner wants such a line
  flagged or dropped, add-on JavaScript can compare the bag with the public product list that PRICES-1118 already reads.
- **CS-05 (info).** "Maximum available" explains a disabled "+" at the stock limit (good); a disabled "-" at one piece has no note, and does not need one
  (the remove button is beside it).
