The Showroom

All 14 cars, rendered on the game's own turntable under the menu's showroom lighting, in the order a player buys them. Every card is the same camera at the same angle, so the sizes are comparable.

What to check

  • 8 of 14 cars share one silhouette. The showroom has 4 body shapes in it — 2× rx7, 3× zx, 8× sedan, 1× gtr — so most of the ladder is the same car in different paint.
  • Gulf Coupe RS كوبيه الخليج is built on the sedan body. The name promises a two-door roofline.
  • Jahra Pickup ونيت الجهراء is built on the sedan body. The name promises a pickup bed.
  • Sharq Hatch شرق هاتش is built on the sedan body. The name promises a hatchback tail.
  • Gulf Coupe RS at 33,000 KD is beaten on every number by Efreet RX at 31,000 KD. There is no reason to buy it.
  • Jahra Pickup costs 6,000 KD and tops out 10 km/h lower than Sharq Hatch at 2,200 KD.
  • Gulf Coupe RS costs 33,000 KD and tops out 10 km/h lower than Efreet RX at 31,000 KD.
