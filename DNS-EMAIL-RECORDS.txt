SPORTA — EMAIL AUTHENTICATION DNS RECORDS (SPF / DKIM / DMARC)
================================================================

WHY THIS MATTERS
Without these records, mail from @sporta.com.kw (order confirmations,
cs@sporta.com.kw replies) can land in spam, and anyone can forge email
that appears to come from your domain. SEO audit tools flag a missing
SPF record because domain trust signals affect brand reputation.

These are DNS records — they are NOT part of the website files and cannot
be uploaded over FTP. Add them in Hostinger: hPanel -> Domains ->
sporta.com.kw -> DNS / Nameservers -> Manage DNS records.

IMPORTANT: You must confirm WHICH service actually sends your mail before
adding SPF. If you send from Hostinger's own email, use the Hostinger
line. If you use Google Workspace / Zoho / Titan, use that line instead.
An SPF record that omits your real sender will cause your own mail to
fail. Check hPanel -> Emails to see which service the domain uses.


1) SPF  — says which servers may send mail as @sporta.com.kw
--------------------------------------------------------------
Type:  TXT
Name:  @            (some panels want "sporta.com.kw" — same thing)
TTL:   14400

Value — pick ONE line matching your mail provider:

  Hostinger email:
    v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com ~all

  Google Workspace:
    v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all

  Zoho Mail:
    v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all

  Titan (common on Hostinger):
    v=spf1 include:spf.titan.email ~all

If you send from TWO services (e.g. Hostinger mailbox + a shop
notification service), combine them in ONE record — never publish two
separate SPF records, that breaks SPF:
    v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com include:_spf.google.com ~all

Notes:
  ~all  = "softfail" — safe starting point; unlisted senders are marked
          suspicious but still delivered.
  -all  = "hardfail" — stricter; switch to this only after a few weeks
          once you have confirmed all legitimate mail passes.


2) DKIM — cryptographically signs your outgoing mail
--------------------------------------------------------------
DKIM keys are generated BY your mail provider — you cannot invent the
value. Get it from the provider, then publish it:

  Hostinger/Titan: hPanel -> Emails -> your domain -> DKIM (it may
                   already be added automatically — check first)
  Google Workspace: admin.google.com -> Apps -> Google Workspace ->
                   Gmail -> Authenticate email -> Generate new record

Type:  TXT
Name:  <selector>._domainkey      (provider tells you the selector,
                                   e.g. "default._domainkey" or
                                   "google._domainkey")
Value: v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=<the long public key the provider gives you>


3) DMARC — tells receivers what to do when SPF/DKIM fail, and
   sends you reports
--------------------------------------------------------------
Type:  TXT
Name:  _dmarc
TTL:   14400

Start in monitor-only mode (takes no action, just reports):
    v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:cs@sporta.com.kw; fo=1

After ~2-4 weeks of clean reports, tighten to:
    v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:cs@sporta.com.kw

Eventually (optional, strictest):
    v=DMARC1; p=reject; pct=100; rua=mailto:cs@sporta.com.kw


ORDER OF WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Confirm your mail provider in hPanel -> Emails.
2. Add SPF (one record only).
3. Add DKIM using the provider's generated key.
4. Add DMARC with p=none.
5. Wait 1-24h for DNS propagation.
6. Verify — send an email to a Gmail address, open it, choose
   "Show original", and confirm all three say PASS:
       SPF: PASS   DKIM: PASS   DMARC: PASS
   Or test at https://www.mail-tester.com (aim for 10/10).
7. Once clean, raise DMARC to p=quarantine.


NOT NEEDED / ALREADY DONE ON THE WEBSITE SIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------
- HTTPS is forced site-wide, with HSTS, in a single 301 redirect.
- Canonical tags, hreflang (en/ar/x-default) and sitemaps are in place.
- robots.txt allows search engines and AI answer engines.
